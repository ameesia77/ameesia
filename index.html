About

Curator, creative producer, artist strategist, and writer. Based in Berlin, independent and available for projects.

Founded AUTOMATA, a gallery exhibiting artists who work with autonomous creative systems and AI agents. Previously Head of Marketing & Curation at Bright Moments, producing exhibitions and cultural programming across ten cities: New York, Berlin, London, Tokyo, Mexico City, Venice, and more (2021–2024). Recent projects include Art Basel, Paris Photo 2025, and writing for Right Click Save.

Currently working as an independent curator and freelance creative producer, expanding across a broader range of contemporary artists — as an artist liaison, strategist, and exhibition producer bringing experimental practices and different mediums together across independent spaces, art fairs, and galleries.

I work in the gray area of contemporary art, digital culture, and unique experiences. The artists I’m drawn to don’t fit neatly into any single category — and I think that’s where the biggest opportunity is right now. I’m creating exhibitions and events that open up what an art experience can be: less defined by transaction, more driven by community, expression, and genuine experimentation. I’m working toward an art world that’s shared, open, and bends the notion of what an artist, an event, or an exhibition can be.
