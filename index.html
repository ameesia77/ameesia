About

Ameesia Marold is an independent curator and creative producer based in Berlin working across digital art, painting, sculpture, performance, and photography, building exhibitions, managing artists, and writing critically about computational creativity.

She co-founded AUTOMATA, a gallery exhibiting artists working with autonomous systems and AI, and previously was head of marketing and events at Bright Moments, where she produced over 30 exhibitions and cultural programming across ten cities worldwide, including New York, Los Angeles, Berlin, London, Tokyo, Mexico City, Buenos Aires, Paris, and Venice (2021–2024). The program culminated in a 216-work auction at Christie’s that she led.

Ameesia’s practice sits deliberately between the digital art world and the traditional one, working to connect different audiences to work that challenges established categories. She is a contributor to Right Click Save, has written for AI Art Magazine, and maintains a regular writing practice on Substack. Recent independent projects include artist management and exhibition production for artists Casey Spooner, Serkan Sarier, Sven Marquardt, and Geoffrey Lillemon.
